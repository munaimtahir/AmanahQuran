# Usability Observations

### Key Usability Strengths
1. **Zero Barrier to Entry**: No account creation, email verification, or permission hurdles stand between the user and the Holy Quran.
2. **Deterministic Navigation**: Users can browse by Surah (114), Juz (30), or printed Page (1-604 / 1-548), supporting diverse traditional memorization and study patterns.
3. **Instant Search**: Search results appear with zero network latency, accepting both Surah names (`Yasin`), Roman transliterations, and verse numbers (`2:255`).
4. **Resilient Local Bookmarks**: Adding and removing bookmarks is instantaneous and reliably persists across app restarts.
5. **Dignified Aesthetics**: The interface prioritizes reverence, tranquility, and textual legibility over gamification or intrusive visual distractions.

### Opportunities for Future Iterations
1. **Reader Context Controls**: Incorporating a compact script switch or typography adjustment dialog directly within the reader would eliminate roundtrips to the Settings screen.
2. **Gesture Affordance Hints**: Displaying a subtle, one-time pulse or indicator on verse cards could help first-time users discover the tap-to-bookmark action card faster.
3. **Home Viewport Optimization**: Slightly compressing the top greeting card on compact screens would allow the Search and Bookmarks buttons to sit above the vertical fold.
