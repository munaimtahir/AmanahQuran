# Al Quran (Tafsir & by Word) — UX & Surface Observations

1. **Home Screen Density**: Features a dense hub with multiple cards (Last Read, Planner, Daily Inspiration, Library). Informative but visually busy.
2. **Word-by-Word Interactivity**: Tapping individual Arabic words shows instant morphological root, lemma, and grammatical case. Unrivaled for active students.
3. **Reader Toolbar**: Fixed top bar with back navigation, Surah title, search, and reader settings. Bottom bar provides audio playback controls and autoscroll toggle.
4. **IndoPak Support**: Supports South Asian IndoPak font alongside standard Uthmani Hafs script with independent font scaling.
5. **Bookmark Architecture**: Complex separation of 'Pins' (quick resume markers), 'Bookmarks' (saved verses), and 'Library Collections' (custom study lists).
