# Al Quran (Tafsir & by Word) — UX Weaknesses & Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- **High Cognitive Load**: Too many competing features (Planner, Streaks, Library, Audio, Tafsir, Word analysis) crowd the interface.
- **Complex Navigation**: Readers can easily lose track of whether they are in Study mode, Mushaf mode, or an isolated bookmark collection.
- **Settings Overload**: Over 60 individual toggles and preferences arranged in deep hierarchies make basic customization daunting for elder users.
- **Distracting from Pure Reading**: For users who simply want to engage in sacred, quiet recitation (Tilawat), the analytical chrome can feel overwhelming.
