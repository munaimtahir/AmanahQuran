# Tarteel: AI Quran Memorization — UX Weaknesses & Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- **Severe Monetization Pressure**: Aggressive paywalls and upsells interrupt the spiritual reading journey.
- **Privacy & Telemetry**: Collects user voice data, tracking IDs, and engagement analytics via commercial SDKs.
- **Anxiety-Inducing Gamification**: Loss-aversion streak mechanics can create spiritual guilt and pressure.
- **Online Dependency**: Heavy voice AI recognition requires cloud network connectivity.
