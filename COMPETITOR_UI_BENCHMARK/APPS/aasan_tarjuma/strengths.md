# Aasan Tarjuma-e-Quran — UX Strengths & Adoptable Patterns

- **Elder Demographic Suitability**: High contrast, readable Nastaliq fonts, and simple navigation make it elder-friendly.
- **Scholarly Authority**: Mufti Taqi Usmani's translation is universally accepted across diverse theological schools.
- **Offline Reliability**: Bundles full translation and Arabic text offline from first launch.
- **Focused Simplicity**: Avoids social feeds, shopping, gamification, and unnecessary permissions.
