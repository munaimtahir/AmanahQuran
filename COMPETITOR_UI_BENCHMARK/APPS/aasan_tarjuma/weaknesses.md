# Aasan Tarjuma-e-Quran — UX Weaknesses & Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- **Zero English Support**: Purely monolingual Urdu, excluding diaspora youth and international readers.
- **Multi-Tap Navigation**: Requires 3-4 taps through index hierarchies to jump to a specific verse reference.
- **Auto-scroll Jitter**: Auto-scrolling can be uneven on variable-length commentary verses.
- **Limited Customization**: Theme choices are restricted to basic light, dark, and green tint.
