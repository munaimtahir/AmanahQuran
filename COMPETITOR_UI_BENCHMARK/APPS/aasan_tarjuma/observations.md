# Aasan Tarjuma-e-Quran — UX & Surface Observations

1. **Urdu-First Interaction**: Tailored specifically for Urdu-literate readers, especially elder demographics in Pakistan and South Asia.
2. **Ruku-Based Reading**: Retains traditional Ruku divisions, reflecting how South Asian readers structure their daily and Taraweeh readings.
3. **Generous Default Typography**: Urdu Nastaliq is rendered with proper baseline spacing, preventing diacritic clipping.
4. **Zero Commercial Ads**: Fully ad-free charitable app focused entirely on transmission of Mufti Taqi Usmani's work.
