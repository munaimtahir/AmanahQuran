# The Glorious Quran — UX Strengths & Adoptable Patterns

- **Content Authority**: Matches Amanah Quran's primary translation objectives (Irfan-ul-Quran and Manifest Quran).
- **Clean Theological Focus**: Respectful presentation without commercial or social distractions.
- **Bilingual Flexibility**: Readers can toggle between Arabic+Urdu, Arabic+English, or all three simultaneously.
- **South Asian Reader Resonance**: High adoption and emotional attachment among readers seeking explanatory Urdu.
