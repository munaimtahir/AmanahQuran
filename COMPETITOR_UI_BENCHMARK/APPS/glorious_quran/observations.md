# The Glorious Quran — UX & Surface Observations

1. **Direct Translation Pairing**: The core benchmark for Amanah Quran's translation context. Displays Dr. Muhammad Tahir-ul-Qadri's Irfan-ul-Quran (Urdu) and The Manifest Quran (English) with exceptional theological fidelity.
2. **Typography Hierarchy**: Distinct styling separates Arabic divine text, modern Urdu Nastaliq commentary, and English modern phrasing.
3. **Auto-Scroll Engine**: Integrated auto-scroll allows hands-free reading during personal devotions or listening.
4. **Waqf / Ad-Free Ethos**: Completely free publication without commercial banners or monetary interruptions.
5. **Search Limitations**: Keyword search in Urdu requires exact character matching without aggressive stem normalization.
