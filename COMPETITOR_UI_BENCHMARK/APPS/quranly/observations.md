# Quran by Quranly — UX & Surface Observations

1. **Habit-First Architecture**: Built entirely around behavioral habit loops (Cue -> Routine -> Reward -> Investment).
2. **Celebratory Animations**: Completing a reading session triggers confetti, streak counter increment, and motivational quotes.
3. **Minimalist Aesthetic**: Pastel palettes, generous padding, and modern rounded card containers.
4. **Subscription Paywall**: Free tier is severely limited; frequently prompts users to subscribe to unlock full routines and unlimited streak freezes.
5. **Account Dependency**: Cloud synchronization is mandatory to maintain streak history.
