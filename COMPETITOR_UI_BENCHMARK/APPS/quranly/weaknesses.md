# Quran by Quranly — UX Weaknesses & Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- **Trivializes Sacred Contemplation**: Gamified streaks and confetti animations risk turning Quran recitation into a superficial Duolingo-like chore.
- **Guilt & Anxiety Patterns**: Push notifications and streak-loss alerts cause emotional fatigue.
- **Subscription Friction**: Commercial barrier blocks basic Islamic habit tracking.
- **Lack of Deep Quranic Navigation**: Poorly suited for serious Juz/Hizb study or scholarly tafsir reading.
