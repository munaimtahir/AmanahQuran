# Quran Majeed – القران الكريم — UX & Surface Observations

1. **Super-App Clutter**: Features dozens of disconnected services (prayer, compass, TV, shopping, ads) crowding out the core Quran reading experience.
2. **Severe Ad Disruption**: Full-screen video ads with sound can trigger while opening a Surah, creating an unacceptable breach of sacred decorum.
3. **Pakistani User Expectation Baseline**: Despite ads, remains hugely popular in Pakistan because its 15-line IndoPak printed layout matches the physical Mushafs memorized by millions.
4. **Heavy Monetization SDKs**: Contains Google AdMob, Unity, AppLovin, and in-app purchase prompts up to $99.99.
