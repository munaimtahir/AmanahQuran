# Quran Majeed – القران الكريم — UX Weaknesses & Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- **Worst-in-Class Ad Intrusions**: Commercial video popups and banner ads completely destroy spiritual tranquility.
- **Overwhelming Visual Chaos**: Cluttered home screen creates cognitive fatigue and slows time-to-Quran.
- **Privacy & Battery Drain**: Multiple commercial advertising networks track device IDs, location, and analytics.
- **Fragile State Persistence**: User reviews frequently complain about lost bookmarks and corrupted audio files after updates.
