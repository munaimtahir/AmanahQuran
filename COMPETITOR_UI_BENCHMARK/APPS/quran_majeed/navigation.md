# Quran Majeed – القران الكريم — Information Architecture & Navigation Map

- **Home Surface (Extreme Density)**: Banner ads, Prayer times widget, Athan alert, Hijri calendar, Qibla compass, Live Makkah/Madinah TV, Halal guides, Quran audio player, Surah/Juz index.
- **Reader Modes**:
  - *IndoPak Printed Mushaf Mode*: Exact photographic reproduction of classic Pakistani 15/16 line printed pages.
  - *Uthmani Text Mode*: Digital font rendering.
  - *Translation Mode*: Verse-by-verse list with multiple Urdu/English options.
- **Ad Interruption Surfaces**: Interstitial full-screen video ads on opening surahs, sticky bottom banner ads under the Quran text, native promotional cards in indices.
