# Quran for Android — UX & Surface Observations

1. **Zero-Distraction Mushaf View**: Default reader presents pure page graphics replicating the printed Madani complex Mushaf. No buttons or text encroach on the page unless tapped.
2. **Post-Install Network Friction**: Cold launch halts at 'Download Required Files?' dialog. Core offline reading is blocked until assets finish downloading over the network.
3. **Disabled Offline Search**: Searching '2:255' or 'Yasin' before downloading a translation database yields an empty list with a 'GET TRANSLATIONS' call to action.
4. **Gesture Ergonomics**: Paging respects Arabic Right-to-Left orientation (swiping left-to-right advances to the next page). Intuitive for experienced Mushaf readers, but can confuse beginners.
5. **Night Reading Mode**: High-contrast inverted night mode with comfortable sepia and dark options.
