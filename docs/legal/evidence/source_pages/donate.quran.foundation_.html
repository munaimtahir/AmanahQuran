Our Latest Achievements

Highlights of New Features, Tools, and Content from the Past Year

This year, Quran.com was significantly enhanced to provide a more powerful, accessible, and beneficial experience for readers at every stage. Through improved study tools, expanded global access, and stronger infrastructure, we have continued building a platform that serves millions worldwide with excellence and care, insha'Allah.

The Quran.com reader was redesigned to support deeper engagement and meaningful study. We introduced Study Mode, enhanced navigation, improved collections, a new verse comparison tool, layered translation, and integrated related hadith references. Notes and collections were also refined to help users organize, reflect, and return to their learning with ease.

Nineteen new Quran translations were added this year, including underserved languages, expanding access to millions more readers. Word-by-word translation was expanded to six languages, and a dedicated Vietnamese experience was launched. These efforts reflect our continued commitment to making the Quran accessible, understandable, and meaningful for diverse global communities.

Significant infrastructure improvements were made this year to ensure faster performance, stronger stability during peak times such as Ramadan, and greater reliability for millions of users worldwide. These enhancements allow Quran.com and its services to scale sustainably while maintaining a smooth, uninterrupted experience for our growing global community.

Structured Learning Plans received a highly positive response this year, with thousands of enrollees and more than 3,000 five-star ratings from users who found them meaningful and beneficial. These guided journeys help users connect real-life challenges such as screen use, emotional resilience, and spiritual growth to Quranic guidance. Combining verses, scholarly insights, reflection-provoking questions, and practical application, Learning Plans aim to move users from passive reading to sustained, transformative engagement.

Content rooted in established scholarship continued to expand, including new surah introductions and question-based tafsir materials. A translation correction and feedback process was also established to continuously improve accuracy and quality. By combining scholarly depth with thoughtful design, Quran.Foundation remains committed to making authentic knowledge accessible without compromise.

We built a new content management system (CMS) for Quran.com, a major milestone that significantly accelerates our ability to serve users. Previously, publishing new lessons required technical timelines. Today, our team can create, review, and publish Quran-centered content across multiple languages much faster, helping more people access guidance, reflection, and learning when they need it.

A major new version of QuranReflect was released across web, iOS, and Android, featuring a redesigned homepage, improved discovery, enhanced feeds, and a more intuitive experience. Over 1 million visitors engaged with the platform, with thousands of reflections written and shared as users connected their lives to the guidance of the Quran.


Beyond the platform, live reflection retreats, podcasts, and interactive sessions fostered meaningful engagement. Twelve monthly themes, including initiatives such as #RamadanReady, #LightForGaza, #TadabburTaughtMe, and other campaigns that encouraged users to engage with the Quran around real-life concerns and global events. Alhamdulillah, QuranReflect continues to grow as a safe, carefully moderated space for thoughtful reflection and shared learning.

To maximize impact and avoid duplication, Quran.Foundation actively engages with organizations, scholars, and communities across the global Quran ecosystem. Through participation in gatherings such as the Quran Summit in London, Aqwam Qeela in Doha, Muslim Tech Fest, and collaborations with partners such as Al Sharq Youth, the team works to listen, align efforts, and identify opportunities for collective impact. This approach ensures that resources are used wisely and that the work strengthens the broader global effort to serve the Quran.

This year marked major growth in tools that extend the Quran's reach beyond our own platforms.


Key initiatives included:

  • Launch of the Quran Apps page
  • Expansion of Quran Labs
  • Secure APIs with OAuth support
  • The Ayah Embed Builder, enabling websites and platforms to embed accurate Quran text, translations, and audio with proper attribution

These tools enable developers, educators, and organizations worldwide to responsibly build Quran-centered experiences, multiplying impact far beyond a single platform.

To support growing global demand, Quran.Foundation expanded its international team across engineering, leadership, and content, with contributors spanning multiple regions. Every addition was made thoughtfully to ensure responsible stewardship of donor funds while strengthening our ability to serve more users effectively. Work also began on a comprehensive rebranding initiative to advance the mission and further growing global role and impact, insha'Allah.
